# Using NPD Tracker (simple guide)

This is an easy, step-by-step guide for using the app. No tech knowledge
needed!

> **Note:** this is the *new* version of NPD Tracker. It's not set up for
> everyday use yet — ask whoever manages the app whether you should be using
> this one or the older one.

## 1. Opening the app

1. Make sure your computer is connected to the same network as the computer
   that runs the app.
2. Open your web browser (like Chrome).
3. Type in the address you were given.
4. That's it! You don't need to download or install anything.

## 2. Signing in

You'll see two ways to sign in. Use whichever one you were told to use:

- **Sign in with Google** — click the button and pick your Google account.
- **Username and Password** — type in the username and password you were
  given, then click Sign in.

## 3. Looking at products

When you sign in, you'll see a big list — one row for each product. At the
top there are colored boxes showing how many products are in each stage
(like "New Product," "Activated / Live," and so on).

You can:
- **Search** — type in the search box to find a product by name or supplier.
- **Filter** — use the dropdown boxes to only show certain statuses, or only
  Active (or Inactive) products.
- **Sort** — click a column title (like "Date" or "Cost") to sort the list by
  that column. Click it again to flip the order.
- **Checklist** — the small colored boxes show the steps for getting a
  product ready to sell. Green means done, gray means not done yet. Hover
  your mouse over them to see what each one means.
- **⚠ Stuck** — if a product has been sitting in the same status for a month
  or more, you'll see a little "Stuck" warning next to its status. Just a
  gentle reminder to check on it.

## 4. Adding a new product

1. Click the orange **+ New Product** button (top right).
2. Fill in the boxes. The ones with a little red star (*) must be filled
   in — the rest you can leave blank and fill in later.
3. For the **Supplier** box: click it to see a list of suppliers to choose
   from. If the supplier isn't in the list yet, click the **+** button next
   to the box, type the new name, and click Add.
   - Once you've picked a supplier already on the list, the box locks so you
     don't accidentally change it by mistake. Click the little **×** to
     clear it if you picked the wrong one.
4. For **Date** boxes: click them to open a little calendar and pick a date.
5. When you're done, click **Save Product**.

**Adding photos:** you can only add photos *after* the product has been
saved once (the app needs to know which product the photos belong to). Save
the product first, then open it again to add photos — see the next section.

## 5. Editing a product (and adding photos)

1. Click anywhere on a product's row (or click the pencil ✎ icon).
2. Change whatever you need to change.
3. Scroll down to **Photos** — there are two separate photo areas:
   - **Product Photos** — regular pictures of the product.
   - **Nutrition Label Photos** — pictures of the nutrition label.
   Click **+ Add** in either one to upload a photo from your computer or
   phone. You can add more than one photo to each area. Hover over a photo
   and click the **✕** in the corner to remove it (it'll ask you to confirm
   first).
4. Click **Save Product**.

There's also a **History** button at the top of the edit screen — click it
to see everything that's ever changed on that product, and who changed it.
Click **Edit** to go back to changing things.

## 6. Deleting a product (and undoing it!)

1. Click the trash icon 🗑 next to a product (or the **Delete** button inside
   the edit screen).
2. It will ask "Are you sure?" — click **Delete** to confirm.

**Made a mistake?** No problem! Click the **Recently Deleted** button at the
top of the page. You'll see a list of everything deleted recently. Click
**Restore** next to the one you want back, and it comes right back.

## 7. Importing and exporting

- **Export CSV** — downloads the whole list as a file you can open in Excel.
- **Import CSV** — lets you add lots of products at once from a file. Click
  it, choose your file, and the app will show you a preview first — so you
  can check everything looks right before anything is actually saved. Tip:
  if you're not sure how to format your file, click **Export CSV** first to
  get a file with the exact right column layout.

## If something doesn't work

- **Can't open the page at all** — make sure you're on the same network as
  the computer running the app, and that the computer is turned on.
- **The page loads but you can't sign in** — double-check the address you
  were given is typed exactly right.
- **"This Google account is not authorized"** — your Google account isn't on
  the allowed list. Try the username/password option instead, or ask
  whoever manages the app to add you.
- **Still stuck?** — ask whoever manages the app for this team for help.
